## Texts
- Shota Rustaveli, *ვეფხისტყაოსანი*, and Vazha-Pshavela, poems, public domain, from
  [ka.wikisource.org](https://ka.wikisource.org)
- English: *The Man in the Panther's Skin*, translated by Marjory Scott Wardrop (Royal Asiatic Society,
  1912), public domain, from the scan at [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.546153)
- English Vazha-Pshavela: George Hewitt's translations, *not bundled*. They are free to read but not
  licensed for redistribution, so in English mode each machine downloads them once from
  [georgehewitt.net](https://www.georgehewitt.net/pdf/Translations_from_Vazha_Pshavela.pdf) into
  `~/.cache/pharnavos/`. Until then (or offline), the portrait is drawn from the English Rustaveli
